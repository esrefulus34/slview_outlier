IOC Sea Level Monitoring - FAQ & Formulation

Outlier Detection Mechanism

All values \( X \) where \( |X - \text{median}| > \text{tolerance} \) are flagged and hidden.
tolerance = 3 × |percentile90 - median|
The statistics (\(\text{median}\) and \(\text{percentile}_{90}\)) are calculated on the data being plotted (12h, 1d, 7d, 30d).

Spike Removal

For spike removal, we take the median out of a window with size 3.

What the 'Offset' Value Represents

The offset is the average (mean) across the current time frame that we subtract to make sure the graph evolves around zero. This allows aligning multiple sensors with different offsets on top of each other.

Vertical Datum Information

IOC only deals with relative sea level data and does not have the vertical datum information directly available. For referenced data, contact the station operator or consult the Long-term MSL data link.
